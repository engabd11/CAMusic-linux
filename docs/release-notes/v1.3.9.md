**Timing that moves the timing in use.**

## Fixed

- **Timing on the Lights page sets the timing HueGhost is using.** 1.3.8 picked the music timing whenever the lights were in Music mode, but HueGhost uses the music timing only while a song plays. A film in Music mode had its number snap back, and a song in Video mode still did. The panel now asks HueGhost which timing is live; this needs HueGhost 2.13.5. With an older HueGhost, Timing sets the film timing, as before 1.3.8.
