**Album and artist pages worth reading.** The pages for a record and for an artist now tell you about them, after the Android app's Gallery pages, and the lyrics under the record have more room.

## New: the album page

- **The record slides out of its sleeve.** Open an album and its vinyl slides out from behind the cover, the label printed in the cover's colours. While the album is playing, the record turns.
- **The cover's colours** as a row of dots under the title, and the artist's name in the album's colour: tap it to go to their page.
- **About this album.** The album's notes from Jellyfin (tap to read on), and the facts set out like a sleeve: released, label, genre, tracks, length, discs and rating, where your library knows them.
- **More by the artist** and **You might also like**, two shelves of records to go to next.

## New: the artist page

- **Their picture across the top.** Where Jellyfin has a backdrop for the artist, it fills the top of the page and drifts as you scroll, with their logo (or their name, large) and a line of facts: albums, the years they span, their genre.
- **Latest release**, as a wide card in its own colours.
- **Through the years.** The discography along a timeline, oldest first, the year marked wherever it changes.
- **About the artist** (their biography) and **Similar artists**.

## Changed

- **Lyrics on the Vinyl layout** show four lines: the line just sung, the line being sung, and the two after it. The title sits a little lower to make room.
- **The karaoke fill** is clean white over the unsung words; the glow behind it drew a box on the station's screen and is gone.
- **Cover Flow** stands larger in portrait.
- **The Home carousel** only shows records that have a cover.
