**The hi-fi station.** This release gives the panel things to touch and things to read, for a station that plays all day: a turntable that is actually playing the record, the words of the song, a crate of records to dig through, and the care a screen needs when it never turns off.

**Everything here is an option, and every option is off until you switch it on.** Nothing that was in 1.1 has been replaced. Open **Settings → Hi-fi station**, or pick the new layout under Listening mode, and the panel looks and behaves exactly as before until you do.

## Fixed

- **The cover no longer jumps when a title wraps.** In the portrait Listening view, a title that ran to two lines pushed the cover up, and it moved again on every track change between a short and a long title. The words now sit in a slot of their own below the cover and grow downward into it.

## New: the turntable

On the **Vinyl** layout (Settings → Hi-fi station):

- **Tonearm.** At rest it waits off the edge of the record. When play starts it swings out and lowers onto the lead-in with the record already turning, rides inward as the side plays, and on pause lifts and returns to its rest. Grooves show where each track begins, with the playing one lit in the album's colour. The "side" is the album's run of the queue; a lone track is a smooth record with no rings.
- **Touch the tonearm.** Take the arm in a finger and set it down on another track, or part-way into one. Only the arm takes touches; the rest of the screen still wakes the panel.
- **Queue deck.** The next three records lean beside the platter, each with the edge of its record showing. Tap one to play it; drag one over its neighbour to change the order.

## New: lyric sheet

A fifth **Listening mode layout**. The song's words roll large down the screen, the line being sung bright at the eye and the lines around it falling away, set in the lettering the album already gets from its genre. Timed lyrics follow the music line by line; plain ones drift down at the pace of the track. With no lyrics for a track it shows a quiet rest and the layout still looks finished.

Lyrics come from Jellyfin. A track queued from this panel is looked up by its Jellyfin id; one queued by another MPD client is found by title and artist.

## New: keepsakes

- **Postcards.** Hold the cover in the Listening view to save a postcard to `~/Pictures/CAMusic`: the cover, the title in its own face, the signal line (`FLAC • 96/24 → your DAC`) and the date, on a portrait card. A flash and a line of text confirm it.
- **Back of the sleeve.** Hold the cover in the player and it turns over like a sleeve in the hand: the album's name and facts, and its tracks with the playing one lit. Touch a track to play it. A tap turns it back, and it turns itself back after 45 seconds.

## New: touch

- **Touch gestures.** Swipe the cover left or right to skip. Swipe a queue row left to take it off the queue, or drag it by its new handle to move it. Drag the cover's right edge for volume, where MPD has a volume to set (a fixed bit-perfect output has none, and then there is no rail).
- **Row menus.** Hold a song in the library, or a row in the queue, for a menu: Play next, Add to queue, Go to album and Go to artist for a song; Play now, Play next, Move to the end and Remove for a queue row.
- **Crates.** A new way into the library, beside the grid: albums sorted into bins by genre, the records standing in them tilted as they pass the middle, the one in front raised with its name on the lip of the bin. Drag a bin along; tap a record and it is pulled out and opened.

## New: the signal

- **Signal path.** Source, decoder, loudness, volume and output as lit stages, an amber lamp on any stage that alters the audio, and a **BIT-PERFECT** lamp when nothing MPD reports does. In the listening view it sits under the title; in the landscape player it replaces the one-line readout; in the portrait player, tap the quality badge to open it. It reads only what MPD and the library report, so it cannot see inside an ALSA plugin or a DAC driver. With ReplayGain on (the default is album) the lamp will say so rather than light.
- **Sleep timer.** A chip beside the loudness chip steps through End of album, 30, 60 and 90 minutes. End of album asks MPD to stop after the last track of the album has played out, so the record is not cut off, and the stylus lifts and parks. (On an MPD too old for that, it pauses as the last track ends.)
- **Big readouts.** Times and the clock set large, for reading from across the room.

## New: a screen that never turns off

- **Connection banner.** When MPD or Jellyfin cannot be reached, a calm banner across the top says which, with a Retry button, instead of a small line of status text. MPD must miss three polls in a row before it counts as down, so a dropped packet does not flash it.
- **Deep idle.** After a long time untouched (15 minutes to 2 hours) the panel goes to a dim clock that moves to a new place each minute, so nothing is lit in one spot for long — or switches the screen off with X's power saving (`xset dpms`). A touch wakes it and does not press what is underneath. Screen off is a power-saving state, not a hardware backlight control, and does nothing on a Wayland session.

## Not in this release

- The library is not cached for browsing while Jellyfin is down; the banner says it is unreachable, and what is playing carries on.
- Live level meters. A bit-perfect output cannot be tapped for levels, and the panel does not draw ones it cannot measure.

## Upgrade or install

Same as 1.1: `sudo ./install.sh` from the tarball replaces the copy in `/opt/camusic-linux`, and your settings in `~/.config/camusic/settings.json` carry over unchanged. Fourteen new keys are added to that file the first time you switch something on; none of them is written until then.
