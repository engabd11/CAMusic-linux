**Colour as the point.** This release makes the panel more of a picture for a listening station that is on all day and all night: a grade for every record's colours, new shapes for the living liquid, new ways for the Listening view to lay out the record, and a night mode. It also fixes pause sometimes needing two or three taps.

## Fixed

- **Pause works on the first tap.** The Listening view (which only appears while music plays) kept catching touches as it faded out, so the tap after the one that woke it did nothing. It also caught taps while it was still fading in. Now it only takes a touch once it is fully on screen. A tap while it fades in goes straight to the button you can see, and the view steps aside. The album gallery follows the same rule.
- **Play and pause read the player at the tap.** The button decides from MPD's answer right then, rather than a reading up to a second old. It turns over the moment MPD takes the command, and quick taps are sent in order.

## New: Settings → Colour & finish

- **Colour mood** grades every album's colours: the accent, the liquid, the blooms, Listening mode and the screensaver. The covers themselves never change.
  - **Natural:** the album as it is.
  - **Warm:** tungsten amber, like a lamp-lit room.
  - **Cool:** moonlight blues.
  - **Mono:** a duotone in the album's leading hue.
  - **Neon:** the colours turned right up.
- **Vignette** (Soft, Deep) darkens the edges like a framed print.
- **Film grain** (Fine, Rich) lays a still film texture over everything. It also smooths the banding an LCD shows in soft gradients, and it is re-laid every minute so nothing marks the screen.
- **Night** warms the whole panel toward lamplight and dims it, easing in and out over twenty minutes. Run it **on a schedule** (22:00 to 07:00 by default, both adjustable) or **always**, at a **Gentle** or **Deep** depth.

## New: Liquid styles

Under **Settings → Now playing & motion → Living colours**, choose the shape the album's colours flow in:

- **Marble:** inks folding through water (as before).
- **Aurora:** curtains hanging and swaying from the top.
- **Nebula:** soft smoke curling like a distant cloud.
- **Lava:** slow blobs rising and merging, like a lava lamp.
- **Silk:** fine ribbons with a sheen.

## New: Listening view layouts

Under **Settings → Listening mode → Layout**:

- **Classic:** the cover beside its words (as before).
- **Vinyl:** the record on the platter with its cover as the label. It turns at 33⅓ while the music plays, coasts to a stop on pause, and the room's light stays put while the grooves turn under it.
- **Poster:** the title set huge like a gig poster, with the cover small in the corner.
- **Cover only:** just the artwork, large, in its own light.

Everything is off or unchanged by default, so the panel looks exactly as it did until you pick something.

## Upgrade or install

```bash
tar -xzf CAMusic-linux-1.1.0-x86_64.tar.gz
cd CAMusic-linux-1.1.0-x86_64
sudo ./install.sh            # replaces the copy in /opt/camusic-linux; your settings are kept
camusic
```

Your settings in `~/.config/camusic/settings.json` carry over unchanged.
