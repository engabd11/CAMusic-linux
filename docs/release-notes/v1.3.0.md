**The gallery.** This release makes the panel something to look at as much as something to use: the library as a wall of covers, the words of the song filling in as they are sung, a radio that keeps the music going, and motion that flows from one screen to the next.

**These are on from the start.** Each one is still a switch, in **Settings → Gallery and lyrics** and **Settings → Listening mode**, and none of them touches the sound.

## New: browsing the library

- **A carousel on Home.** New records and a few to rediscover, one at a time and large, on their own art blurred into the page, with Play and Open. It moves on every few seconds; swipe for the next.
- **Cover Flow.** The Albums page has a Grid and a Flow switch at the top. Flow stands one record facing you with the rest turned on their edges either side, a reflection on the floor beneath. Drag or fling through them, tap the middle one to open it, tap a side one to bring it round. Left alone, it turns a record every few seconds, so the panel becomes a slow show of your library.
- **Covers grow into their page.** Tap a cover in a grid, a shelf, the carousel or the flow, and that cover grows into the album page; Back shrinks it home again.
- **Motion that flows.** Pages and tiles rise into place as a page opens, tiles give a little under the finger, the album page's backdrop drifts with depth as it scrolls, and tabs sink back and come forward instead of cutting.
- **Faster covers.** Grids and shelves ask the server for covers at the size they are drawn, and covers are kept on disk (`~/.cache/camusic/art`), so long grids scroll smoothly and a restart does not fetch the library's art again.

## New: lyrics, karaoke style

- **The words fill as they are sung.** In the Lyric sheet layout, the line being sung fills word by word in white with a glow of the album's colour, and the focus glides from line to line. Lyrics with word timing (enhanced LRC, or Jellyfin 10.11's word cues) follow the voice exactly; the rest are spread across each line by length.
- **Lyrics in every Listening mode layout.** Classic, Vinyl, Poster and Cover show the line being sung and the next one under the record. Switch it off in **Settings → Listening mode → Lyrics under the record**.
- **Lyrics in the player.** A new **Lyrics** chip beside the loudness chip turns the cover into the song's words. Tap the words to turn it back.
- **Lyrics found online.** When Jellyfin has no timed lyrics for a song, the panel asks [LRCLIB](https://lrclib.net), an open lyrics library. It sends the title, artist, album and length. Switch it off in **Settings → Gallery and lyrics → Find lyrics online**.

## New: radio

- **Start a radio** from an album page or an artist page (the new radio button beside Shuffle), from a song's hold menu, or from the new **Radio** card on Home. The music that follows is like what you started from: Jellyfin's instant mix first, then the artist's best known songs, then the genre, then the whole library.
- **It keeps going.** A few songs before the queue runs out, the radio adds more, avoiding anything it has already played in the session and spreading the artists out.
- **A chip says so.** "Radio · Kind of Blue" shows in the player and on the Queue page while a radio runs; tap it to stop. Playing an album or clearing the queue stops it too.

## New: the art wall

A fourth screensaver style, **Art wall** (Settings → Screensaver → Style). The library's covers fill the screen in columns that drift slowly past each other. Every few seconds one record steps into the light, the rest dim, and its name comes up at the foot of the screen. Hold to play it.

## Upgrade or install

Same as before: `sudo ./install.sh` from the tarball replaces the copy in `/opt/camusic-linux`, and your settings in `~/.config/camusic/settings.json` carry over. The new switches use their defaults until you change them.
