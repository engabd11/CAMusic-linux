**Smooth all the way through.** A pass over the motion and the small details, so the station feels like one instrument rather than a set of screens.

## Motion

- **Progress glides.** The seek bar, the mini player's progress line and the listening view's hairline now move with every frame, carried on between MPD's once-a-second readings, instead of stepping each second. They rest when the music does.
- **The seek knob** swells under the finger and settles back with a little give.
- **Tabs** have one lit pill that slides to the chosen tab, its icon lifting as it arrives.
- **Choices** (every Off / Track / Album style switch) have one accent thumb that slides to the chosen option.
- **The queue moves as a list.** Remove a song and the ones below glide up into the gap; songs the radio adds fade in. The Now playing card crosses over to the next song.
- **The mini player's** words rise in on a track change, and its buttons give under the finger.
- **Buttons, rows and cards** all sink a touch under the finger and spring back.
- **The listening view** settles in from slightly larger as it fades up, like the lights going down.
- **Scrolling** stops cleanly at the end of a list after a fling.

## Details

- **The connection line** under the tabs is now two small lit dots, MPD and Jellyfin, when everything answers. The words come back, in amber, only when something needs saying.
- **Radio on the Queue page** is a strip under the header, saying what it started from, with Stop. The header no longer crowds.
- **Read more** on an album's notes or an artist's biography shows only when there is more to read.
- **The Home carousel and Cover Flow** stop turning while the listening view or the screensaver covers them, so the panel does no work nobody can see.
