**Covers after a restart.** A fix for the now-playing cover, on top of [1.3.0, the gallery](https://github.com/engabd11/CAMusic-linux/releases/tag/v1.3.0).

## Fixed

- **The cover shows when the music was already playing.** When the panel starts with MPD already playing (after a reboot, an upgrade, or an album queued from another app), the cover now comes from MPD itself: the picture embedded in the file, or the cover image beside it. Before, the now-playing screen, the listening view and the room's colours showed a plain placeholder until an album was queued from the library.

Everything in 1.3.0 is here too: the Home carousel, Cover Flow, covers that grow into their page, karaoke lyrics in every listening layout and in the player, radio, and the Art wall screensaver.
