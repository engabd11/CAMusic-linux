**Hue light sync that stays on the beat.** This release fixes the position the panel reports to Jellyfin, which HueGhost follows to put your lights on the music.

## Fixed

- **The reported position no longer drifts away from the music and jumps back.** MPD moves its play position in steps (a quarter of a second, playing bit-perfect 96 kHz). The panel checked it every second plus a moment, so each reading landed a little later in the step than the last. Jellyfin saw the song fall behind by about 2.4 ms every second, then jump 0.2 s forward every minute or so, and lights following it slid in and out of the beat. The panel now checks every 1.037 s, so its readings cover the whole step, and it reports where the music really is, within about 40 ms, all the time.

Nothing to set: it works as soon as the panel runs this version. It pairs best with HueGhost 2.12.0, which also locks each song once its start is confirmed and then leaves it alone.
