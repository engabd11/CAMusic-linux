**Living colours.** On the panel, the 1.0.0 "living cover" (the cover's own pixels moving) read as shimmer rather than motion. In 1.0.1 the cover stays perfectly still, and the life moves into the room around it.

## What's new

- **The album's colours flow like a liquid.** With **Settings → Now playing & motion → Living colours** on, four of the record's colours fold through each other behind the player the way inks mix in water. The effect is strongest behind the cover and settles toward the controls, so the words stay easy to read.
  - **Subtle:** soft, broad and slow.
  - **Vivid:** crisper bands, deeper swirls, a quicker flow.
- **It wakes with the music.** About 10 s into each track (adjustable under *Starts flowing after*) the liquid gathers speed. When you pause it slows to a stop and holds the mix; it never jumps.
- **Everywhere the room is lit.** The liquid shows behind the now-playing cover, behind the library, queue and settings pages, and as the background of **Listening mode**. Each album starts from its own pattern, and the colours blend across when the album changes.
- **Off** brings back the previous soft colour blooms.

Everything else from 1.0.0 is unchanged: the steady album art, the album-change flip, Listening mode, the screensaver, the library and the lights.

## Upgrade or install

```bash
tar -xzf CAMusic-linux-1.0.1-x86_64.tar.gz
cd CAMusic-linux-1.0.1-x86_64
sudo ./install.sh            # replaces the copy in /opt/camusic-linux; your settings are kept
camusic
```

Your settings in `~/.config/camusic/settings.json` carry over unchanged. The Subtle/Vivid choice you made in 1.0.0 now drives the living colours.
