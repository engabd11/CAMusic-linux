**Jellyfin always knows what is playing.** Two fixes reported from the station.

## Fixed

- **Music that was already playing is reported to Jellyfin.** When the panel started with MPD already playing (after an upgrade, a reboot, or an album queued from another app), it played on but told Jellyfin nothing, so Jellyfin, and HueGhost following it, saw the panel as idle until a new album was queued from the library. The panel now finds those songs in your library, matching each one by its exact file, and reports them within a few seconds. Their covers and album colours come from the library too.
- **Cover Flow reaches every album.** Flow could stop at the end of the albums it had loaded so far (around "Cyberpunk" in a large library) and not load the next ones until something else nudged it. It now loads the rest of your albums in the background as soon as Flow opens, so the count is your whole library and there is no end to run into.
